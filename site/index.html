🌙 Featured Story

Muffin's 4 AM Wake-Up Mission

Every morning at 4 AM sharp, Muffin executes his master plan. Step 1: Walk directly on Dad's face. Step 2: Meow like the house is on fire. Step 3: Sprint into the darkness and knock over everything. Step 4: Return to stare at Dad with those glowing eyes like "You know what you did wrong."

Dad wakes up, terrified and confused. He stumbles to the kitchen, fills the food bowl, and waits. Muffin approaches. Sniffs the food. Takes exactly one bite. Then walks away like a disappointed food critic.

Because here's the thing: Muffin doesn't actually want food. Muffin wants Dad to know who's in charge. And at 4 AM, when everyone is vulnerable, that's when chaos wins. 🐱
