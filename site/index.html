🌙 Muffin’s 4 AM Story

Muffin’s 4 AM Wake-Up Mission

Every morning at 4 AM, Muffin performs his sacred ritual: the “Wake Dad Immediately” operation.

Step 1: Walk across Dad’s face. Step 2: Meow once (dramatically). Step 3: Sprint into the darkness for no reason. Step 4: Return to stare at Dad like a disappointed manager.

Dad wakes up terrified. Muffin just wanted a snack. Or attention. Or simply to cause problems on purpose.

After Dad finally feeds him, Muffin sniffs the bowl, takes one bite… and walks away.

Because the goal was never food — the goal was chaos.
